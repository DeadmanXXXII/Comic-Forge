# Comic Forge — Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 26 September 2026*

## Summary
Comic Forge does not have a server, does not have user accounts, and does
not collect, store, or transmit your data to us. Everything the app does
happens inside your own browser, on your own device.

## What the app stores, and where
The following is saved only in your browser's local storage (`localStorage`),
on your own device:
- API keys you enter for AI image generation providers (OpenAI, Google,
  Hugging Face, Pollinations)
- Story and panel data you write or import
- Generated artwork

This data is never transmitted to Comic Forge's developer or to any server
we operate, because no such server exists. Clearing your browser's site data
for Comic Forge, or using "Clear all keys" / removing the app, deletes it
permanently — we have no copy to restore it from.

## What gets sent to third parties, and why
When you generate artwork, the prompt text for that panel and your API key
for the provider you selected are sent directly from your browser to that
provider's servers (e.g. api.openai.com, generativelanguage.googleapis.com,
api-inference.huggingface.co, or image.pollinations.ai). This is necessary
for the app to function — it is how the image gets generated. That request
goes straight from your device to the provider; it does not pass through us.

Each provider handles that request under their own privacy policy and terms
of service, not ours. Review the provider's own policy for how they handle
prompts, generated images, and API usage:
- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Hugging Face: https://huggingface.co/privacy
- Pollinations: https://pollinations.ai/ (see their site for current terms)

You choose which provider to use, if any, and can use the app without
entering any key at all (the free Pollinations tier requires none).

## What we (the developer) collect
Nothing. We do not operate a backend, analytics, or telemetry for this app.
We have no visibility into your API keys, story content, generated images,
or how you use the app.

## Children's privacy
Comic Forge is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data
from anyone, of any age, because it does not collect data at all.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same URL
