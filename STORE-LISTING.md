# Comic Forge — Store listing description

## Short description (≤100 chars, shows in search results)
Build comics from your story data using your own AI image generation API keys.

## Full description

Comic Forge turns a script or story outline into a laid-out comic — panel by
panel — using AI-generated artwork, with you in full control of how each
image is made.

**How it works**
- Write or paste your story as panels: a description, page number, and panel
  order for each one.
- Pick an AI image generation provider and enter your own API key for it —
  OpenAI, Google Gemini, Hugging Face, or the free Pollinations tier.
- Generate art per panel, regenerate any panel you're not happy with, and
  build a printable comic page from the results.
- Export to PDF straight from your browser's print dialog.

**Your keys, your control**
Comic Forge doesn't run any servers and doesn't have accounts. Every API key
you enter is stored only in your own browser and used only to talk directly
to the provider you chose. Your story content and generated art stay on your
device too. Nothing is sent to us, ever — there's no "us" in the loop between
you and your chosen AI provider.

**Multiple generation backends**
Not locked into one AI provider or one price point. Swap between a free
option and a paid one per panel, compare results, and only pay whichever
provider you're actually using, at their rates — not ours.

**Works offline**
Once loaded, the app shell works without a connection (image generation
itself still needs network access to reach whichever provider you're using).

**Who it's for**
Writers, artists, and hobbyists prototyping a comic or webcomic who want
AI-assisted art without signing up for a bundled paid service — bring your
own key, use as much or as little as you want.

## Age rating notes
No user accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases, no user-generated content
shared with other users (nothing is shared with anyone — it's local-only).
Note in the questionnaire that the app can call third-party AI image
generation APIs the user configures themselves; content produced depends
entirely on what the user prompts and which provider they choose, same as
any other AI art tool.
